About

I like problems that don’t have an answer yet.

I came to product design through games and TV, and I’ve stayed for the moment a confusing thing finally clicks for someone. I live in Vancouver, play hockey, and will happily lose an evening to simulation theory.

Since I was a kid I’ve been curious about where technology and design meet. Now I’m a senior product designer, and the thing teammates mention most is my passion, followed closely by a refusal to accept ‘that’s how we’ve always done it.’
